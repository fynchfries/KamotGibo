# Cinematic one-minute demo revision

## User direction
Keep the existing walkthrough steps and the one-minute duration. Add professional motion graphics, especially click-focused zooms and cinematic tab/view transitions. The previously requested silence and absence of captions remain in force.

## Approach
Post-produce the existing full-resolution captured frames, rather than re-recording or rebuilding the app: this preserves the exact 38-action sequence, ordering, and source timing. A new recording risks changing the steps; a slide montage would stop being a functional walkthrough. Save a separate `kamotgibo-demo-cinematic.mp4` and retain the original.

## Motion treatment
- A restrained dark-teal studio matte, softly lit with brand-compatible teal/clay accents, behind a rounded, subtly elevated app window.
- Smooth, minimum-jerk camera push-ins around important clicks; controlled pans between related controls; readable holds on booking, approval, publication and badge results.
- Short, directional, softly motion-blurred transitions at actual route/view changes. No constant bouncing, flashy wipes, added text, sound, or additional steps.
- The opening establishes the interface; the closing settles back onto the same home screen.

## Deliverable and checks
Exactly 60 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 MP4; one video track, no audio/subtitles. Preserve every source action in order, app files, and the original MP4. Assert motion geometry and transition boundaries; visually inspect close-ups and transitions before the full render; decode/check the final export. All effects are video post-production only, not changes to the application.
